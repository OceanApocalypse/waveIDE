## What is waveIDE?
**waveIDE** is the open-source VSCode alternative that isn't just another fork.
Not only that, but it's also completely telemetry-free and it's free software.

Currently, it's work in progress.

### Motivation
<blockquote>
<p><strong>waveIDE</strong> started out of frustration. I was extremely annoyed by how the .NET ecosystem, despite being cross-platform, was extremely tied to big corporations: outside of Microsoft and JetBrains, good luck finding a good IDE for .NET development.</p>
<p>I began working on waveIDE as a way of taking a break from other time-consuming projects and eventually evolved it into a VSCode-alternative, instead of something targetted specifically at the .NET ecosystem.</p>
<p align="right"><i>- Matthew @ Ocean Apocalypse</i></p>
</blockquote>
